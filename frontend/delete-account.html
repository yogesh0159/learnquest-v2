Delete my LearnQuest account

Done. Your account, every child profile and all their game data were deleted.

You can delete your LearnQuest parent account and all data of your children at any time. Nothing is kept for later.

What is deleted

  • the parent account (name, email, password hash)
  • every child profile (name or nickname, age, class, language, avatar, PIN hash)
  • all game and learning data of those children (coins, points, levels, answers, runs, rewards, tasks)

This cannot be undone. Game settings saved only on a device (when playing without an account) are removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

How to delete (takes one minute)

  1. Open the LearnQuest app, or the parent page of the website.
  2. Choose Parents and log in.
  3. Open Privacy and account at the bottom of the dashboard.
  4. To remove one child, press Delete this child's profile and data. To remove everything, type your password and press Delete my account and all data.

Log in to delete

Cannot log in, or no longer have the app?

Write to chavansir12@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject Delete my LearnQuest account. We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.
