Privacy Policy

LearnQuest Jungle Run  ·  Last updated: 19 October 2026

LearnQuest Jungle Run ("the app" and the LearnQuest website) is a learning game for children. It is made and run by Yogesh Chavan, India ("we"). This page explains in plain words what information the app and the website handle, why, and how you can delete it.

The short version. A parent creates the account; children play through a profile their parent sets up. We collect only what the game needs to work and to show a parent how the child is doing. There are no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and we never sell or share your information. You can delete a child profile, or the whole account, at any time.

1. Who uses the app

The game is made for primary-school children. Children do not sign up on their own. A parent or guardian creates the parent account and adds a profile for the child; by doing so the parent agrees to this policy for the child. You can also play a few free runs without any account: that information stays on the device (see section 3).

2. What we collect

InformationWhy
Parent account: name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way scrambled "hash", we cannot read it)To sign the parent in and show the child's progress to the right person.
Child profile: a name or nickname, age (4 to 12), class (optional), language, avatar, and a 4-digit PIN (also stored only as a hash)To personalise the game and let the child sign in on a shared device. We suggest using a nickname instead of the full name.
Learning and game data: coins, points, level, streak, which questions were answered right or wrong and how long they took, game runs, rewards, and tasks a parent setsTo show the parent the progress, to choose questions that suit the child, and to give rewards.
Technical data: our server briefly uses the network address (IP) of a request to stop abuse (kept in memory, not saved in our database) and writes short logs of what was asked and whether it worked (no passwords, no content)Security and fixing errors. Our hosting provider may also keep standard network logs.

We do not collect: location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone recordings, the advertising ID, phone number or payment information. The app asks Android only for: internet (to reach our server), vibration (a short buzz in the game) and keeping the screen on while a run is going.

3. Playing without an account

Before an account is created, the settings, coins and best scores are saved only on the device (in the app's own storage). They are not sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. How we use the information

Only to run the game and the parent dashboard, to keep accounts secure, and to fix problems. We do not use it for advertising, for marketing profiles, or for anything unrelated to the game. The "Listen" button uses the text-to-speech engine already on the device; we record no sound.

5. Who we share it with

Nobody, except the hosting company that stores the data for us (the server and database run on Railway, acting only on our instructions). We do not sell or rent information, and there are no advertising or analytics companies in the app. We may disclose information only if the law requires it.

6. Security

Information travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Passwords and PINs are stored as hashes. Access to the database is limited to the owner. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a good password and keep your child's PIN private.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep the information until you delete it. You can delete it yourself, at any time:

  • In the app or on the website: Parents → log in → Privacy and account → Delete this child's profile and data or Delete my account and all data.
  • Without the app: open the delete-account page, or write to chavansir12@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, and we will delete the account within 30 days.

Deleting removes the parent, every child profile and all their game data from our live database straight away. Copies kept by the hosting provider for its own backups (if any) disappear when the provider's normal backup cycle ends.

8. A parent's choices

A parent may ask to see the information we hold about their child, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the profile or the account. Write to the address below.

9. Changes to this page

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page before the change goes live and show the new date at the top.
